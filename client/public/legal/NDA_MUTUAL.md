> confidential information **before** a master agreement is in
> place.  Once a master agreement (e.g. an Ojas MSA) is signed, the
> confidentiality clause in that agreement supersedes this NDA.

## 1. Purpose

The Parties wish to explore a potential business relationship
including [briefly describe purpose — e.g. evaluation of the Ojas
service for use by Counterparty, joint product development,
investment discussions] (the "**Purpose**") and, for that purpose,
may exchange Confidential Information.

## 2. Definitions

**"Confidential Information"** means any non-public information
disclosed by one Party (the "**Discloser**") to the other (the
"**Recipient**"), whether orally, in writing, electronically, or by
inspection, that is identified as confidential, marked
"Confidential", or that a reasonable person would understand to be
confidential given the nature of the information and the
circumstances of disclosure.

Confidential Information includes, without limitation: technical
data, trade secrets, business plans, financial information,
customer and supplier lists, pricing, product roadmaps, source
code, security architecture, employee information, and the
existence and content of discussions between the Parties.

**Excluded:** Confidential Information does **not** include
information that the Recipient can demonstrate:
(a) is or becomes generally available to the public without breach
    of this Agreement by the Recipient;
(b) was rightfully known to the Recipient, free of any
    confidentiality obligation, before the Discloser disclosed it;
(c) is rightfully received by the Recipient from a third party
    free of any confidentiality obligation; or
(d) is independently developed by the Recipient without use of or
    reference to the Discloser's Confidential Information.

## 3. Obligations of the Recipient

The Recipient will:

1. Use the Confidential Information **solely for the Purpose**.
2. Hold the Confidential Information in strict confidence and
   protect it with at least the same standard of care that it
   uses for its own confidential information of like importance,
   and in no event less than reasonable care.
3. Disclose the Confidential Information only to its directors,
   officers, employees, agents, and professional advisors who
   (a) have a need to know for the Purpose and (b) are bound by
   written confidentiality obligations no less protective than
   this Agreement.
4. Not reverse-engineer, decompile, or disassemble any software
   or hardware disclosed.
5. Be responsible for any breach by anyone to whom it has
   disclosed Confidential Information.

## 4. Required disclosures

The Recipient may disclose Confidential Information to the extent
required by law, regulation, court order, or other legal process,
provided that (where lawful) it gives the Discloser prompt prior
written notice and reasonable assistance, at the Discloser's
expense, to seek a protective order or other appropriate remedy.

## 5. No license

This Agreement does not grant the Recipient any license, express or
implied, in or to the Discloser's Confidential Information,
intellectual property, or other rights.

## 6. No warranties

All Confidential Information is provided **"as is"**. Neither Party
makes any warranty regarding the accuracy or completeness of
information disclosed.

## 7. Term and survival

This Agreement is effective on the Effective Date and continues for
**two (2) years**, after which it terminates. The confidentiality
obligations in Section 3 survive termination for an additional
**three (3) years** (and, for trade secrets, for as long as the
information remains a trade secret under applicable law).

## 8. Return or destruction

Within **30 days** of the Discloser's written request or
termination of this Agreement (whichever is earlier), the Recipient
will return or destroy all Confidential Information of the
Discloser in its possession or control and, on request, certify
such return or destruction in writing. The Recipient may retain
one (1) archive copy in its legal files solely for compliance
purposes, subject to continuing confidentiality obligations.

## 9. Remedies

The Parties acknowledge that breach of this Agreement may cause
irreparable harm for which monetary damages would be inadequate.
The Discloser is entitled to seek injunctive or other equitable
relief in addition to any other remedies available at law.

## 10. No obligation to continue

Nothing in this Agreement obligates either Party to disclose any
information, enter into any further agreement, or pursue the
Purpose.

## 11. Governing law and jurisdiction

This Agreement is governed by the laws of India. The Parties
submit to the exclusive jurisdiction of {{COURT_JURISDICTION}}.

## 12. General

12.1 **Entire agreement.** This Agreement is the entire agreement
between the Parties regarding the subject matter and supersedes
all prior or contemporaneous understandings.

12.2 **Amendment.** Any amendment must be in writing and signed by
both Parties.

12.3 **No assignment.** Neither Party may assign this Agreement
without the other Party's prior written consent, except to an
acquirer of substantially all of its business.

12.4 **Counterparts.** This Agreement may be executed in
counterparts, including by electronic signature, each of which is
an original and together constitute one agreement.
