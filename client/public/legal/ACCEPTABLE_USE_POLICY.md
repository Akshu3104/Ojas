This Acceptable Use Policy (the **"AUP"**) governs your use of the

violation of this AUP, in line with Section 12 of the Terms.

## 1. Prohibited content and conduct

You may not use Ojas, and may not allow any third party to use
Ojas, to:

### 1.1 Break the law
- Violate any applicable law, regulation, court order, or contract,
  including export-control laws, sanctions, tax laws, consumer-
  protection laws, and data-protection laws (including DPDPA 2023,
  GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA, GLBA).
- Conduct activities that are illegal in India or in the
  jurisdiction where you, your users, or your end-users are
  located.

### 1.2 Harm people
- Generate or distribute content that promotes terrorism, organized
  violence, hate speech, harassment, stalking, doxxing, sexual
  exploitation of any person (especially minors), or
  non-consensual intimate imagery.
- Generate or facilitate child sexual abuse material (CSAM), in any
  form, by any path, ever. We immediately report all such
  attempts to the National Cyber Crime Reporting Portal and to
  NCMEC where applicable.
- Generate or distribute content intended to deceive (e.g. deepfake
  fraud, impersonation of a real person for fraud) or to cause
  reasonable apprehension of physical harm.

### 1.3 Compromise systems
- Probe, scan, or test the security of any system or network
  (including Ojas, your own systems, or third parties) without
  authorization. The Service includes scanning features, but those
  may only be pointed at systems you own or have written
  permission to test.
- Reverse-engineer, decompile, or otherwise attempt to extract the
  source code of the Service, except to the extent expressly
  permitted by applicable law.
- Circumvent or attempt to circumvent rate limits, authentication,
  billing, or other technical controls of the Service.
- Use the Service to develop a competing product through
  benchmarking, scraping, or model distillation. Routine
  competitive analysis is fine; bulk extraction of our outputs to
  train a clone is not.

### 1.4 Misuse credentials
- Share login credentials with anyone outside your organization.
- Use credentials belonging to another person without their
  authorization.
- Sell, sublicense, or resell access to the Service without our
  prior written agreement (which we are happy to discuss — write
  to {{LEGAL_EMAIL}}).

### 1.5 Abuse the network
- Send spam, phishing, or unsolicited bulk communications using
  the Service.
- Use the Service to launch denial-of-service or amplification
  attacks against any system.
- Use the Service as a public proxy for arbitrary HTTPS traffic
  unrelated to LLM gateway functionality.

### 1.6 Manipulate AI safety systems
- Use the Service to generate jailbreaks, prompt injections, or
  evasions targeted at production AI systems other than your own
  for legitimate red-team purposes.
- Use the Service to generate convincing disinformation at scale.
- Use the Service to score, profile, or surveil people in ways
  that would violate applicable law.

### 1.7 Run prohibited high-risk applications
- Critical infrastructure (power, water, dam, gas, nuclear) without
  a separate written agreement.
- Lethal autonomous systems or weapons-control systems.
- Real-time medical-diagnostic or clinical-decision systems where
  output is acted upon without competent human review.
- Real-time law-enforcement decision systems used to detain,
  arrest, or sentence individuals.
- Real-time financial credit decisions without competent human
  oversight.

If you have a specific high-risk use case, contact
{{LEGAL_EMAIL}} — we may be able to support it under a separate
written agreement with additional controls.

### 1.8 Misuse third-party providers
- Violate the terms of service of any upstream LLM provider
  (OpenAI, Anthropic, AWS, Google, etc.) when routing through the
  Ojas gateway. Their terms apply on top of ours.

You retain ownership of Customer Data, but you represent that:
- You have the right to submit the data through Ojas.
- The data does not infringe the IP, privacy, publicity, or other
  rights of any third party.
- You have provided all required notices to, and obtained all
  required consents from, individuals whose personal data is
  contained in the Customer Data.

You are responsible for the lawfulness of your Customer Data and
its use, even if you submit it through Ojas.

## 3. Reporting violations

If you believe someone is using Ojas in violation of this AUP,
report it to **{{ABUSE_EMAIL}}**. Include:
- The behaviour you observed
- The accounts or workspaces involved (if known)
- Any supporting evidence (screenshots, logs)

We investigate every report. Where appropriate, we will respond,
suspend the offending account, and notify the reporter of the
outcome (subject to our privacy obligations to the subject of the
report).

## 4. Enforcement

We may, at our sole discretion and without prior notice:
- Suspend or terminate accounts that violate this AUP.
- Remove or quarantine offending Customer Data.
- Report the violation to law-enforcement or regulators where
  required by law or in good faith to protect users, third
  parties, or the Service.

We will, where reasonable and lawful, give notice and an
opportunity to cure before suspending, except for serious
violations (Section 1.2 CSAM, Section 1.3 attacks, Section 1.7
prohibited use cases) where immediate suspension is required to
prevent harm.

## 5. Updates

We may update this AUP from time to time. Material changes will
be notified by email or in-app banner at least 30 days before they
take effect. Continued use of the Service after the effective date
constitutes acceptance.
