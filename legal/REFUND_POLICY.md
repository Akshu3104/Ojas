This Refund Policy describes when and how **{{COMPANY_SHORT_NAME}}**
refunds payments for Subscriptions to the Ojas service. It
supplements the [Terms of Service](./TERMS_OF_SERVICE.md).

## 1. Free trials

Free trials are exactly that — free. No payment is taken, so no
refund is owed.

If a paid Subscription begins automatically at the end of a trial
and you cancel within the first **14 days** of the paid period, see
Section 2.

## 2. 14-day money-back guarantee (new paid customers)

If you are a **new** paid customer (i.e. your first paid
Subscription), you may request a full refund of the first paid
billing period within **14 days** of the first charge, no
questions asked. Write to {{BILLING_EMAIL}}.

Excluded:
- Usage-based overage fees beyond your plan's included quota are
  not refunded.
- Add-on services purchased as one-offs (e.g. an annual
  professional-services engagement) are not refunded.

## 3. Cancellations on monthly plans

You may cancel a monthly Subscription at any time from your
dashboard's billing settings.

- Cancellation takes effect at the **end of the current billing
  period**. You will not be charged for the next period.
- We do **not** refund unused days within a billing period (this
  is the standard SaaS practice).

## 4. Cancellations on annual plans

You may cancel an annual Subscription at any time, but a refund is
only available in the circumstances below.

- **Within 14 days of the first annual charge**: full refund per
  Section 2.
- **After 14 days**: no refund. Your access continues until the
  end of the paid annual term, and the Subscription will not
  auto-renew at the end of the term provided you cancel at least
  one (1) day before the renewal date.

## 5. Refunds for service failures

You may be entitled to a credit (and in some cases a refund) if
the Service materially fails to perform per the
[SLA](./SLA.md) — see SLA Section 3 (service credits) and SLA
Section 6 (termination for sustained breach).

## 6. Refunds for AUP termination

If your Subscription is terminated under
[Acceptable Use Policy](./ACCEPTABLE_USE_POLICY.md) enforcement,
**no refund is given**. This is intentional — the AUP exists to
keep the Service usable for everyone.

## 7. Refunds for cancellations by {{COMPANY_SHORT_NAME}}

If we terminate your Subscription for convenience (i.e. we decide
to discontinue your tier or your account), we will refund any
pre-paid fees for the unused portion of the Subscription.

## 8. How to request a refund

Email {{BILLING_EMAIL}} with:
- Your account email
- The invoice number(s) for which you are requesting a refund
- A short description of the reason

We aim to respond within **5 business days** and to process
approved refunds within **10 business days** of approval, although
the time for the funds to actually appear in your account depends
on your bank or card issuer.

## 9. Form of refund

Refunds are issued to the original payment method. If the original
payment method is no longer valid (e.g. expired card), we will work
with you to identify an acceptable alternative.

## 10. Taxes

If a refund relates to a charge that included GST or similar tax,
the tax is refunded in proportion. We will issue a credit note in
the format required by Indian GST law.

## 11. Changes

We may update this Refund Policy from time to time. Material
changes will be notified at least 30 days before they take effect.
